Official council portraits are shown on https://www.austintexas.gov/council.
The City serves those image files from its own file storage for that website.

https://www.austintexas.gov/legal-notice says files from Austintexas.gov, including
a file storage facility used by that website, may be used for personal use.
Republishing, resizing, or cropping those files needs written permission from
the City of Austin.

This site does not copy those portraits. Vote displays show initials and names.
Names link to scorecards on this site.
